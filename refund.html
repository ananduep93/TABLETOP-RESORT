Refund & Cancellation Policy

1. Cancellation

Cancellations made 7 days prior to check-in will receive a full refund. Cancellations within 3-7 days will incur a 50% charge.

2. No-Show

No-shows will not be eligible for a refund.

3. Refund Processing

Refunds will be processed within 10-15 business days via the original payment method.
